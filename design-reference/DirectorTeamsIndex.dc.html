A Comp Team is created once and can enter more than one competition over a season — Duo — Ren & Sofia exists but isn't entered anywhere yet, which is normal right after it's created.
Three different things
A Team is the level a dancer is placed in for class — Jazz II, Senior Team. Ongoing, one instructor, holds all season.

A Comp Team (Solo/Duo/Trio/Group) is who competes together on one piece — Take Flight, Duo — Ren & Sofia. Its own roster, often cast across Teams and levels. Exists on its own, independent of any competition.

A Competition is the actual event — Regional Classic 2027, a venue and a date. Comp Teams get entered into it, sometimes more than one, and it's where call times live & get updated as the date gets closer.
