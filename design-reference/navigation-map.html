Dance Studio Messenger V2 · reference

Navigation map

Where a parent or student actually looks, and where the Studio destination fits in relative to it — not a new tab, a pinned row one level in.

1
As a parent or student you have exactly four tabs, always on screen: Home, Schedule, Messaging, Profile. There is no fifth “Studio” tab. Home is a single feed of everything that's yours — if you hold two roles, like Instructor and Parent at once, both show up there with a small chip saying whose item it is, not on two separate screens.
2
Studio isn't special plumbing — it's a virtual destination, built exactly like a Team or a Group Dance, just scoped to everyone instead of one roster. You reach it the same way you'd reach Jazz II: Home → My teams & dances → See all, where Studio is pinned as the permanent first row. Once inside, it has the same five sub-tabs any Team gets — Home, Schedule, Bulletin, Media, Essentials.

Home tab vs. Studio's Home tab

Same tab name, two different jobs — this is usually where the confusion actually starts.

Walked through: Priya's Friday

Priya holds both roles — Parent to Aditi, Instructor for Jazz II — which is exactly where this distinction has to hold up.

  1. She opens the app to Home. It's her personal feed only — Aditi's costume form sits next to a Jazz II roster task, each tagged with a small chip so she knows whose it is. Nothing studio-wide is mixed in here.
  2. A studio-wide post about the recital packing list was marked Important by the Director — so it shows up in her Home's “Needs your attention” automatically, without her going to look for it.
  3. Curious what else is posted, she taps My teams & dances → See all. Studio is sitting at the top of that list, pinned above Jazz II and Aditi's entries — same list, same tap pattern as everything else.
  4. Inside Studio, she lands on its own Home tab — a studio-wide version of the screen she just left — and can flip to Bulletin for the full post history, or Essentials for the packing list itself.

Not drawn above, to keep it readable: the primary Schedule tab works the same way as Home does — it aggregates the Schedule sub-tab of every destination you're in, Studio included, into one calendar. And the Media sub-tab (Studio's and every destination's) is a plain thumbnail library, no relationship worth its own arrow.
