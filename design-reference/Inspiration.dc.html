What HomeUnifiedVisual borrows from each

Fanen (farm-management app) — photo-hero weather header, 3 stat tiles, “My Fields” photo-card gallery
Adopted into Home
Hero The weather photo header becomes a photo hero — greeting overlaid, “Needs your attention” pulled up into a card that overlaps its bottom edge, same way the weather stats overlap in the reference.
Stat tiles Wind / UV / Humidity becomes 3 compact Next-up tiles — icon, bold value, short label. Replaces the old text-heavy scrolling cards.
Photo cards “My Fields”’s photo + status-pill + caption pattern becomes the Highlights gallery — video and photo posts from Directors and dancers.
Adopted into Home
Tile row Band's horizontal “My Bands” row of colorful squircle tiles becomes “My teams & dances” — a photo tile per Team or Dance instead of a plain text row, with a small role badge in the corner (dark+amber ring = Instructor, outlined = Parent) so the role signal survives the more visual format. The trailing dashed tile mirrors Band's “Create/Join a Band” pattern, repurposed as “See all.”
